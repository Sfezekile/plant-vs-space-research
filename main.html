This project explores whether plant-like life could exist beyond Earth, combining science with creative design. You’ll produce a report and an immersive website, each worth 50 marks. The report covers plant survival needs, two worlds (Mars, Europa, Titan, Kepler-442b, Proxima Centauri b), a speculative alien plant design, scientific search methods, and a reflection with credible sources. The website mirrors this structure, styled like a space documentary with dark visuals, NASA imagery, responsive layouts, and at least one interactive JavaScript feature. Together, the deliverables blend research and imagination to show how plants might adapt under alien conditions and how scientists search for signs of extraterrestrial vegetation.
